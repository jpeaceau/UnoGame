Uno:
Select between 2 and 10 players.

Each player will automatically receive 7 cards to start with, and the first registered player takes the first turn,
in order of the players registered, but is subject to reverse with the reversal card.

A card can be played if either: They share the same colour, or number.

There are ten primary cards, from 0 to 9. Only one 0 card exists per colour, while two 1-9 cards exist per colour.
There are 3 action cards (skip, reverse, draw 2) per colour.

Skip: Skips the next player's turn, or the current player if the starting card is a skip.
Draw Two: Skips the next player's turn, and they draw 2 cards, or the current player if the starting card is a draw two.
Reverse: Reverses the order of turn progression. If there are only two players, this behaves the same as skip.

Moreover, there are 2 types of wild cards: wild, and wild draw four.

Wild: The player who played the card or first player where the first card is wild, selects a colour that the card will
be.

Wild draw four: The same as wild, but the next player draws 4 cards and has their turn skipped. However,
this can be challenged.

Challenge: When a wild draw four is played, the player on the receiving end can challenge. This results in the player
who played the draw four to expose their hand. If there was another card that matched the previous face card, they
must draw 4 cards, and the challenger retains their turn.

If the challenge is unsuccessful, the challenger picks up 6 cards instead and still misses their turn.

Note that a player can still have another card with the same number as the face card, and still legally play the draw
four card.

The first player to empty their hand of cards wins.
